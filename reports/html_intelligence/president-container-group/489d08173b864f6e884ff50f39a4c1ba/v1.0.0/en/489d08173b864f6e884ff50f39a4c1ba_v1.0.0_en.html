01

The audit protects the investment decision.

PCG has an exceptional production base. At this scale, improving physical evacuation alone, without measuring the constraints that feed and receive it, may move the bottleneck rather than remove it.

Technical thesis Before freezing the conveyor, pallet-insertion, restacking, strapping and wrapping design, INGECART proposes measuring the complete cycle from each converter's final production point to finished-goods release. The result will be a vendor-neutral specification PCG can share with all suppliers.

INGECART commitment Deliver immediate minimum-spend, maximum-output actions and the design, control and integration recommendations needed to capture the highest possible return from the future solution.

03

A fast conveyor does not correct a slow sequence.

Shifted bottleneck

The new outfeed clears faster, but strapping, wrapping, restacking, quality control or pickup saturates next.

Disconnected capacity

Equipment sized on average rate fails under peaks, large bundles, incomplete pallets, exceptions or recipe changes.

Control islands

PLCs and drives that could be automated remain isolated; priority, destination and recovery decisions stay manual.

Misread buffer

More accumulation may hide the problem, increase WIP and delay detection of a stopped downstream process.

Common availability

A shared architecture without bypass or degraded mode can turn one local fault into several stopped converters.

Unreconciled units

Boxes/h, bundles/h, pallets/h and ft2/h are not interchangeable without recipe, count, pattern and mix.

Decision question Which combination of capacity, buffer, redundancy and software keeps each converter below its acceptable blocking limit during foreseeable peaks and failures?

Size by event, concurrency and percentile.

Transparent example: one EVOL

At combined theoretical peak, one common constrained minute represents at most 1,200 boxes. The audit will replace that maximum with observed good speed and cause-coded minutes.

05

Three lines are not a capacity validation.

Strapping sufficiency must be proven against p95 demand and actual mix, including corner-board placement, double straps, changeovers, rejects and availability. The working hypothesis is to assess a fourth line or replace current technology with automatic strappers and automatic corner-board insertion.

3 lines challenged

Operating experience indicates insufficient capacity. This must be documented through queues, blocked time, utilization and failure recovery.

Fourth strapper

Adds capacity and redundancy if routing, space, labor and pickup can distribute the load.

Automatic + corner boards

Removes manual tasks from the critical cycle and can sharply increase the final station's sustainable rate.

Correct interpretation of 400-600% A net 400-600% improvement means the new station would produce 5 to 7 times baseline output if “improvement” is incremental. The audit will define the convention, time current and automatic cycles by SKU, and prevent a commercial claim from becoming a guarantee without FAT/SAT.

06

Three days, four disciplines, one flow model.

Day 1 · Gemba, baseline and loss taxonomy

Map routes and states; time cycles; capture formats, bundles and pallets; identify starvation, blocking, microstops, rehandling, operator waits and accumulation. Align tags and causes with production, maintenance and quality.

Day 2 · Electrical, PLC, drives and connectivity

Inventory PLC/HMI/VFD/servo assets, versions, networks and protocols; review I/O, handshakes, safety, alarms, histories and data capability; verify interfaces across bundlers, restackers, pallet inserters, strappers, wrappers and conveyors.

Day 3 · Stress test, quick wins and target architecture

Observe peaks and changeovers; test agreed recovery and manual modes; validate queues and constraints; prioritize immediate actions; close functional requirements, missing data and investment acceptance tests.

INGECART team

Technical direction, engineering direction, electrical and software engineering, and project direction. Coordinated participation with PCG production, maintenance, IT/OT, quality and safety.

Capture method

Direct observation, time study, history exports, event counts, structured interviews, available drawing and program review, authorized technical photography and end-of-day reconciliation.

09

PLCs execute; the orchestrator coordinates.

Load contract

load_id, order, SKU, quantity, dimensions, weight, pallet/no pallet, bottom layer, strapping, wrapping and destination.

State contract

ready, running, starved, blocked, fault, manual, occupied, destination reserved and pickup requested.

Exception contract

destination full, off-center load, missing pallet, jam, equipment down, network loss, E-stop and recovery.

Design principle No load leaves without identity and reserved destination; no destination receives without confirmed capacity; every exception retains traceability and has a safe route, buffer or degraded procedure.
