7. Visit Methodology

Each preventive maintenance visit follows a structured methodology developed over years of experience:

  1. Pre-visit planning: Review of previous reports, open actions, and customer priorities. Preparation of required tools and spare parts.
  2. On-site inspection: Systematic mechanical and electrical inspection following a standardised checklist covering all critical systems.
  3. Preventive interventions: Lubrication, adjustment, calibration, and replacement of consumable items according to schedule.
  4. Measurement and testing: Vibration, temperature, current, and functional testing to baseline and trend performance data.
  5. Findings report: Detailed report of findings, actions taken, corrective recommendations, and parts required.
  6. Debriefing: On-site meeting with maintenance/operations team to review results and align on priorities.
