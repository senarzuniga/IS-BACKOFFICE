1. Executive Summary

The most suitable technical basis for the future IAR product is a UWB-first architecture, reinforced by RFID identity checkpoints and prepared for controlled hybrid expansion. This direction best satisfies precision, latency, robustness, and scalability requirements in corrugated paper roll warehouses.

Economic evidence shows meaningful differences in entry CAPEX and recurring OPEX models across suppliers, making 5-year TCO normalization mandatory before final award.

1. Resumen Ejecutivo

La base tecnica mas adecuada para el futuro producto IAR es una arquitectura UWB-first, reforzada con checkpoints RFID de identidad y preparada para expansion hibrida controlada. Esta direccion satisface mejor precision, latencia, robustez y escalabilidad en almacenes de bobinas de carton corrugado.

La evidencia economica muestra diferencias relevantes en CAPEX de entrada y modelos de OPEX recurrente entre proveedores, por lo que es obligatoria la normalizacion TCO a 5 anos antes de adjudicar.

2. Operational Requirements

  • Slot-level location confidence under dense stacking.
  • Near-real-time updates for forklift operations and search-time reduction.
  • Reliable behavior under metallic multipath and intermittent occlusion.
  • Valid Z-axis performance for stacked rolls at different heights.
  • Manageable calibration and maintenance effort for industrial continuity.
  • Scalable architecture for growth in tags, zones, and process complexity.
  • Industrial integration capability for MES transaction consistency.
  • Readiness for future digital twin and AI-enabled evolution.

2. Requisitos Operacionales

  • Confianza de posicion a nivel hueco bajo estiba densa.
  • Actualizacion cuasi en tiempo real para operaciones de carretilla y reduccion del tiempo de busqueda.
  • Comportamiento fiable bajo multipath metalico y oclusion intermitente.
  • Rendimiento valido del eje Z con bobinas apiladas a distintas alturas.
  • Carga de calibracion y mantenimiento gestionable para continuidad industrial.
  • Arquitectura escalable para crecimiento de tags, zonas y complejidad de proceso.
  • Capacidad de integracion industrial con consistencia transaccional MES.
  • Preparacion para evolucion futura a gemelo digital e IA aplicada.

6. Engineering Trade-Off Analysis

6. Analisis de Trade-Offs de Ingenieria

  • Centralized vs distributed: centralized simplifies governance; distributed improves latency and fault containment. Recommended: distributed acquisition with centralized reconciliation.
  • Single-vendor vs abstraction layer: single-vendor accelerates the initial deployment, but abstraction protects the roadmap. Recommended: abstraction from phase 1.
  • Active vs passive tags: active tags provide continuous location; passive tags only optimize identity cost.
  • Hybrid technologies: increase integration complexity, but improve resilience and evolution.
  • Fixed anchors vs mobile infrastructure: fixed anchors provide repeatability; mobile infrastructure adds flexibility with more uncertainty.
  • Cloud vs edge: edge for deterministic latency; cloud for analytics and history.

7. Recommended Architecture for INGECART IAR

7. Arquitectura Recomendada para INGECART IAR

  • Hardware: fixed UWB anchors and active tags as the positioning backbone; RFID for checkpoints; optional vision in critical zones.
  • Communications: deterministic local telemetry network and event backbone for movement, inventory, and exceptions.
  • Positioning: real-time engine with confidence scoring, outlier filtering, and health diagnostics.
  • Middleware: vendor abstraction API and unified roll-state model.
  • MES interface: bidirectional inventory, movement, and reconciliation contracts.
  • Future digital twin interface: timestamped state stream and replay-ready history.
  • Future AI modules: search optimization, forklift flow optimization, congestion prediction, predictive maintenance.

8. Technology Selection

Selected technology: UWB as the primary indoor positioning technology, under a hybrid-ready architecture.

Why: best overall balance of precision, latency, industrial robustness, and operational utility.

Expected risks: site-specific multipath, calibration drift, and cost escalation if licenses/support are not normalized early.

Validation required: same-site multi-vendor benchmark, full-shift tests, Z-axis reliability, MES consistency, and 5-year TCO scenarios.

8. Seleccion Tecnologica

Tecnologia seleccionada: UWB como tecnologia primaria de posicionamiento indoor, sobre una arquitectura preparada para hibridacion.

Por que: mejor balance global entre precision, latencia, robustez industrial y utilidad operativa.

Riesgos esperados: multipath especifico de sitio, deriva de calibracion y escalada de costos si licencias/soporte no se normalizan desde el inicio.

Validacion requerida: benchmark multi-vendor en mismo sitio, pruebas de turno completo, fiabilidad del eje Z, consistencia MES y escenarios TCO a 5 anos.

9. Conclusions

UWB-first is the strongest technical path for the future IAR product. A hybrid-ready architecture with technology abstraction provides the best long-term balance of robustness, scalability, and flexibility. Final supplier award should be made only after harmonized technical validation and normalized 5-year economic comparison.

9. Conclusiones

UWB-first es la via tecnica mas robusta para el futuro IAR. La arquitectura hibrida con abstraccion tecnologica ofrece el mejor balance de robustez, escalabilidad y flexibilidad a largo plazo. La adjudicacion final de proveedor debe realizarse solo tras validacion tecnica armonizada y normalizacion economica completa.
