- Automatic return of unused or partially used reels to assigned storage locations.
- Designed to remove uncontrolled forklift circulation in the corrugator area.
- The simulation materials treat reel logistics as a capacity and safety bottleneck rather than a simple transport task.

Technical deep-dive:
- Automated movement of reels/paper rolls in corrugated plants.
- Designed for integration with warehouse logic and plant flow control.
- Digital twin validation is used to compare logistics alternatives.
- **ASSUMPTION:** exact payload, conveyor geometry, and cycle-rate figures require field validation from the final project configuration.

Industrial application:
- Corrugator reel rooms and non-automated roll warehouses.

Engineering benefits:
- Less forklift traffic.
- Faster replenishment of the corrugator.
- Better line continuity and lower handling risk.

Differentiation:
- More than a transporter: it is an engineered logistics layer with simulation-backed validation.

### 6.11 RFID / GPS / RTLS Positioning
**EN**
The RTLS corpus is strong and technically clear. It shows that indoor roll positioning cannot rely on GPS as a primary core, and that the best architecture is UWB-first, supported by RFID for identity continuity, with BLE AoA or vision as optional complementary layers. The business case is traceability, reduced search time, and resilient indoor positioning.

Technical signals recovered from the source corpus:
- RTLS is an indoor system for locating, identifying, and managing rolls where GPS is not valid.
- RFID is valuable for non-contact roll identification at critical pass points and stations.
- UWB is the strongest primary indoor technology for continuous location.
- BLE AoA can work as a complementary medium-precision layer; BLE RSSI is coarse only.
- Vision is useful for semantic context but is sensitive to occlusion, dust, and lighting.

Technical deep-dive:
- UWB as the primary indoor core.
- RFID for identity checkpoints and transaction continuity.
- GPS RTK for outdoor boundary use only.
- BLE AoA / RSSI as complementary layers.
- Vision as an augmenting semantic layer.

Industrial application:
- Roll warehouses, reel movement, station validation, and traceability.

Engineering benefits:
- Better identification of rolls.
- Stronger indoor traceability.
- Lower operational ambiguity.

Differentiation:
- Hybrid positioning designed for corrugated reality, not consumer-grade tracking.
