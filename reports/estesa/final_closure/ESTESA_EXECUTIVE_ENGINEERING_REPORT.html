1. Executive Summary

ESTESA is a family-owned company founded in 1979 with exclusive representation for Spain and Portugal of major machinery manufacturers in the corrugated and paperboard sectors. INGECART is an independent industrial engineering specialist with 28 years of experience, 1,268 projects, 26 international agreements, and 194 active installations.

The combined opportunity is to create a premium engineering-commercial platform for corrugated, packaging, intralogistics, robotics, and industrial digitalisation. ESTESA contributes market access and OEM credibility. INGECART contributes plant diagnosis, integration, commissioning, and measurable performance improvement.

1. Resumen Ejecutivo

ESTESA es una empresa familiar fundada en 1979 con representación exclusiva para España y Portugal de fabricantes de maquinaria líderes en corrugado y cartoncillo. INGECART es una ingeniería industrial independiente con 28 años de experiencia, 1.268 proyectos, 26 acuerdos internacionales y 194 instalaciones activas.

La oportunidad conjunta es crear una plataforma comercial y de ingeniería premium para corrugado, packaging, intralogística, robótica y digitalización industrial. ESTESA aporta acceso al mercado y credibilidad OEM. INGECART aporta diagnóstico de planta, integración, puesta en marcha y mejora de rendimiento medible.

6. Detailed Product Technical Descriptions

Note: Where exact cycle rates, payloads, dimensions, or control-stack details were not published in the recovered sources, the report labels them as ASSUMPTION and recommends field validation.

INGETRANS

Automated reel feeding and transport family for corrugated production. Strong digital-twin validation context; reduces forklift dependency and stabilizes reel flow to the corrugator.

  • Floor-mounted rail transport from warehouse to consumption points.
  • Automatic supply to multiple corrugator consumption points.
  • Automatic return of unused or partially used reels to storage.
  • Designed to remove uncontrolled forklift traffic in the corrugator area.

AMR Intralogistics Waste Management

AMR-based transport for waste and scrap flows with flexible routing. Best used where fixed conveyors are too rigid or where waste routes change by shift or product mix.

AMR Intralogistics WIP Management

AMR movement of work-in-progress between process stages. Reduces congestion and manual transport while improving flow synchronisation.

Full Waste Control with AMR Intralogistics and Weighing Stations for Reels In and Reels Out

Integrated control concept linking reel weighing, waste transport, and material identity into a single accountability loop.

Heavy Duty Palletizer

Robust end-of-line palletizing solution for heavy loads and demanding duty cycles; KUKA-backed automation logic strengthens reliability and service support.

  • Targeted at lines above 400 pieces/min or multiple-outs.
  • Automatic box collection and palletizing for high-speed converting.
  • Tailor-made robotic palletizer and conveyor system per machine take-off.

Plug and Play Palletizer

Pre-engineered palletizer for quicker deployment and lower integration overhead. Best for customers needing a standardised path into automation.

  • Stable production capacity of 12 bundles/minute.
  • Maximum pallet height of 2300 mm.
  • Palletizing area up to 1200 × 1200 mm.
  • Universal servo-driven gripper for more than 50 bundle formats.
  • Automatic pallet discharge and compact footprint.

SR-1400

Continuous waste collection and transport system; the sources support a claim of up to 93% lower energy consumption versus conventional alternatives.

  • Fully sealed ramp-chain design.
  • Variable-speed drives to adapt evacuation flow dynamically.
  • Positioned as a strategic waste-management layer rather than an auxiliary conveyor.

Digital Warehouse

Real-time roll warehouse visibility and full traceability for non-automated roll warehouses.

  • Real-time location and status visibility.
  • Reduced search times and elimination of manual inventories.
  • Improved stock accuracy.
  • RTLS design assumption up to 12 m warehouse ceiling height.

Industrial Digital Twin

Simulation-based validation environment to compare logistics alternatives before capex is committed.

  • Discrete-event simulation for logistics benchmarking.
  • Physical-process twins for tension, pressure, and temperature.
  • Kinematic simulation for sequence optimisation and collision avoidance.

AI Engineering Platform (ING_DIGHUB)

Engineering intelligence layer that orchestrates knowledge, diagnostics, and AI-assisted decision support.

  • Operational agents detect anomalies in pressure, vibration, temperature, and waste.
  • Maintenance agents predict failures and prioritise orders by OEE impact.
  • Commercial and customer-success agents identify adoption value and upsell signals.

RFID / GPS / RTLS Positioning

UWB-first indoor positioning with RFID identity continuity; GPS RTK is outdoors only and BLE layers are complementary.

  • RTLS for indoor roll identification, tracking, and station validation.
  • UWB as the primary continuous indoor positioning technology.
  • BLE AoA as a complementary layer; BLE RSSI only for coarse presence.
  • Vision as a semantic complement, sensitive to dust and occlusion.
