# ESTESA Executive Product Annex

Date: 2026-07-24
Scope: INGECART solutions relevant to corrugated and packaging modernization.

## Product Sheet 1: INGETRANS

- Engineering purpose: automated reel logistics from warehouse to corrugator consumption points.
- Industrial application: high-traffic reel rooms with forklift bottlenecks and safety exposure.
- Technical characteristics: rail-based transport, automatic reel supply, automatic reel return, digital-twin validation capability.
- Competitive advantages: deterministic feed to corrugator, lower forklift dependency, better continuity.
- Integration possibilities: Digital Warehouse, RTLS, Full Waste Control, Industrial Digital Twin.

## Product Sheet 2: AMR Intralogistics Waste Management

- Engineering purpose: flexible mobile transport for scrap and waste flows.
- Industrial application: plants where fixed conveyors are too rigid for changing routes.
- Technical characteristics: AMR routing, dynamic mission assignment, segregation-friendly logistics.
- Competitive advantages: flexible deployment, scalable by route complexity, lower manual transport load.
- Integration possibilities: Full Waste Control, SR-1400, RFID/RTLS checkpoints.

## Product Sheet 3: AMR Intralogistics WIP Management

- Engineering purpose: WIP flow balancing between process stages.
- Industrial application: line-to-line transfers, temporary buffers, pre-palletizing staging.
- Technical characteristics: dynamic routing, stage synchronization, configurable transfer logic.
- Competitive advantages: less congestion, lower manual handling, improved flow predictability.
- Integration possibilities: palletizing cells, Digital Warehouse, AI Engineering Platform.

## Product Sheet 4: Full Waste Control with AMR Intralogistics and Weighing Stations for Reels In and Reels Out

- Engineering purpose: closed-loop accountability of input reels, output reels, and residual scrap.
- Industrial application: plants requiring strict material reconciliation and trim-loss control.
- Technical characteristics: reel-in/reel-out weighing, AMR transport events, RFID/RTLS identity continuity.
- Competitive advantages: auditable traceability, lower unaccounted loss, stronger KPI governance.
- Integration possibilities: Digital Warehouse, SR-1400, AI Engineering Platform.

## Product Sheet 5: Heavy Duty Palletizer

- Engineering purpose: high-throughput robotic end-of-line palletizing for demanding duty cycles.
- Industrial application: multi-out converting lines, high-speed output, heavy load patterns.
- Technical characteristics: robot-based load formation, conveyor-cell integration, KUKA-enabled support ecosystem.
- Competitive advantages: repeatability, lower ergonomic risk, industrial serviceability.
- Integration possibilities: Plug and Play Palletizer family, KUKA stack, digital performance analytics.

## Product Sheet 6: Plug and Play Palletizer

- Engineering purpose: fast deployment palletizing with standardized configuration.
- Industrial application: customers requiring quick automation adoption with controlled scope.
- Technical characteristics: 12 bundles/min stable production, 2300 mm max pallet height, up to 1200x1200 mm area, over 50 bundle formats.
- Competitive advantages: short commissioning horizon, reduced integration overhead, predictable rollout.
- Integration possibilities: line take-off conveyors, AMR WIP transport, Digital Twin validation.

## Product Sheet 7: SR-1400

- Engineering purpose: continuous engineered waste logistics for corrugated environments.
- Industrial application: lines with sustained trim/scrap generation and high evacuation demand.
- Technical characteristics: sealed ramp-chain architecture, variable-speed drives, continuous evacuation design.
- Competitive advantages: up to 93% lower energy consumption claim versus alternatives, cleaner flow, robust duty profile.
- Integration possibilities: AMR Waste Management, Full Waste Control, AI KPI tracking.

## Product Sheet 8: Digital Warehouse

- Engineering purpose: real-time visibility and traceability of paper rolls in non-automated warehouses.
- Industrial application: reel warehouses with search-time and stock-accuracy gaps.
- Technical characteristics: location/status visibility, full roll traceability, transaction continuity.
- Competitive advantages: lower search time, better stock accuracy, reduced material-loss risk.
- Integration possibilities: RTLS, RFID stations, INGETRANS orchestration.

## Product Sheet 9: Industrial Digital Twin

- Engineering purpose: pre-investment validation of logistics and process alternatives.
- Industrial application: capex planning, layout reconfiguration, risk reduction before execution.
- Technical characteristics: discrete-event simulation, physical-process twin logic, kinematic analysis.
- Competitive advantages: stronger ROI evidence, lower implementation risk, faster decision confidence.
- Integration possibilities: INGETRANS, palletizing cells, AMR route design.

## Product Sheet 10: AI Engineering Platform (ING_DIGHUB)

- Engineering purpose: decision intelligence layer for operations, maintenance, and optimization.
- Industrial application: complex plants requiring faster diagnostics and cross-role coordination.
- Technical characteristics: multi-agent pattern for operations, maintenance, optimization, management, commercial and customer success.
- Competitive advantages: shorter troubleshooting cycles, better knowledge reuse, stronger consistency.
- Integration possibilities: all core products through KPI, alarms, and recommendation workflows.

## Product Sheet 11: RFID / GPS / RTLS Positioning

- Engineering purpose: robust indoor traceability with identity continuity and location confidence.
- Industrial application: roll tracking, station validation, warehouse movement governance.
- Technical characteristics: UWB-first indoor positioning, RFID for checkpoints, GPS RTK for outdoor boundary only, BLE AoA as complementary layer.
- Competitive advantages: lower ambiguity, stronger traceability, resilient indoor operation.
- Integration possibilities: Digital Warehouse, Full Waste Control, INGETRANS, AI platform.
