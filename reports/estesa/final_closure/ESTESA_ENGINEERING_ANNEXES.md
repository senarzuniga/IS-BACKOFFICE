# ESTESA Engineering Annexes

Date: 2026-07-24
Project: ESTESA x INGECART strategic engineering partnership

This annex package complements the main report and centralizes product, architecture, evidence, and scoring information for executive and technical review.

## Annex Structure

1. Executive Product Annex
- File: ESTESA_EXECUTIVE_PRODUCT_ANNEX.md
- Purpose: product-level executive sheets with engineering intent, technical characteristics, and integration role.

2. Architecture Annex
- File: ESTESA_ARCHITECTURE_ANNEX.md
- Purpose: reference architecture, flow logic, integration interfaces, and future evolution paths.

3. Technology Comparison Matrix
- File: ESTESA_TECHNOLOGY_COMPARISON_MATRIX.md
- Purpose: side-by-side technology alternatives, strengths, limitations, and deployment implications.

4. Scoring Matrix
- File: ESTESA_SCORING_MATRIX.md
- Purpose: weighted technical scoring, confidence, and recommendation ranking.

5. Evidence Index
- File: ESTESA_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md
- Purpose: traceability map from engineering claims to source documents and authority level.

6. Image Annex
- File: ESTESA_IMAGE_ANNEX.md
- Purpose: available visual assets (diagrams, brochures, product visuals, and media references).

7. Asset Manifest
- File: ESTESA_ASSET_MANIFEST.md
- Purpose: publication package manifest and deliverable inventory.

## Use Guidance

- Executive readers: prioritize the main report, scoring matrix, and recommendation sections.
- Engineering readers: prioritize the product annex, architecture annex, and evidence index.
- Commercial teams: prioritize synergy matrix, roadmap, and product application sections.
