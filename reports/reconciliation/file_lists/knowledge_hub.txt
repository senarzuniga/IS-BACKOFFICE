Knowledge hub and competitive intelligence (knowledge_hub/ and agents/knowledge_intelligence)

- knowledge_hub/competitive_intel/* (many modules: truth_engine.py, streamlit_ci_panel.py, parser.py, indexer.py, hybrid_retrieval.py, etc.)
- agents/knowledge_intelligence/orchestrator.py
- agents/knowledge_intelligence/agents/* (web_research_agent.py, validation_agent.py, technical_writer.py, reviewer_agent.py, etc.)

Notes:
- These modules orchestrate multi-agent flows. They require LLM keys and environment variables to run in full mode.
- Outputs and job artifacts live under `data/competitive_intelligence/jobs/` and `knowledge_hub/outputs/`.
