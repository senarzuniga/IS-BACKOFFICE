Automated Corrugator Paper Reel Feeding System

1Text

Automated Corrugator Paper Reel Feeding System INGETRANS is a complete automated solution for managing paper reel supply to multiple consumption points on the corrugator, including the automatic return of unused, or partially used, reels to their assigned storage locations. Designed and manufactured by Ingecart, this system eliminates forklift dependency, reduces accident risks, and significantly improves operational efficiency. The KEY 👉 With the available operational data, a clear conclusion emerges: the real bottleneck is not the travel speed of the forklift, but the availability, synchronization, and coordination of reel supply. INGETRANS delivers its value not by moving faster, but by transforming reel handling into a continuous, predictable, and automated process. This results in fewer supply interruptions, improved corrugator availability, higher OEE, enhanced operational safety, and a more stable and efficient logistics flow throughout the plant.
